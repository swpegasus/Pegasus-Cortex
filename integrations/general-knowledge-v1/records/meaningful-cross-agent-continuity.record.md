Agents should preserve continuity as a compact operational state, not as a replay of the conversation.

At the beginning of meaningful work, recover the exact current task and only the few retained items relevant to the present intent. Maintain a concise task body containing the objective, verified current state, material uncertainty or blocker, and next action. Update it only when one of those facts meaningfully changes.

Retain a separate knowledge record only for a durable correction, preference, failure, regression, workaround, decision, observation, or broadly reusable tenet. Generalize reusable conclusions into the appropriate shared namespace while keeping project evidence and personal preferences in their narrower namespaces. At checkpoint, block, completion, or handoff, commit the task transition and related learning atomically when the repository supports it.

Do not retain greetings, formatting-only work, trivial facts, repeated checks, raw command output, full transcripts, private reasoning, secrets, or speculative filler. Exact record bodies should be loaded only when needed; bounded summaries and typed references are the normal agent payload.
